Your first gym plan: 3 full-body days a week

A simple, proven way to start lifting: three short sessions, the big movements, and steady progress.

If you're new to the gym, you don't need a complicated split. Training your whole body three times a week, with a rest day between sessions, gives every muscle enough practice and enough recovery.

The plan

Day A (Mon)Day B (Wed)Day A (Fri)
Goblet squat · 3 × 10Romanian deadlift · 3 × 10Goblet squat · 3 × 10
Dumbbell bench press · 3 × 10Dumbbell shoulder press · 3 × 10Dumbbell bench press · 3 × 10
Seated cable row · 3 × 12Lat pulldown · 3 × 12Seated cable row · 3 × 12
Plank · 3 × 30 sWalking lunges · 2 × 10 each legPlank · 3 × 30 s

The next week starts with Day B, so the two workouts keep alternating.

How heavy?

Pick a weight you could lift two or three more times at the end of each set. When you get all your reps on every set with good form, go up a little next time.

Warm up and rest

Five minutes of easy cardio and a light first set of each exercise. Rest about 90 seconds between sets.
