Logging

Every set on one screen.

Set, previous, weight, reps and a box to tick. Start the timer with one tap, or just tick the box when you're done.

  • Weights picked from your level and last session
  • Plate math per side for every bar and plate machine
  • Rest timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
  • Minimize the break and look ahead whenever you like

Plans

A plan that adapts to you.

Tell Apex your goal, your week, your equipment and the muscles you want to focus on. It builds the plan and rebuilds it every 5 weeks from how you actually trained.

  • Six goals, 2 to 6 days a week
  • Pick focus muscles: they get extra sets and exercises
  • Free weights or a machine? Switch in one tap
  • Build, peak and deload weeks for steady progress

Voice coach

Hear exactly what to do.

One calm, natural voice in English, Portuguese or Spanish. It explains each new exercise once, tells you which muscle to feel and lets you know when the break is over.

  • The next exercise is announced when your break ends, not during it
  • Lines never talk over each other
  • Your music stays loud if you prefer
  • Turn each kind of line on or off in Settings

Progress

Keep the streak alive.

A day streak that understands lifting: your rest days count, and a streak freeze covers the day life gets in the way.

  • Every personal record celebrated on the spot
  • Level 1 to 5, set from what you lift
  • InBody and smart-scale results imported automatically
  • A reminder at 8 PM if today's session is still to do
