Questions

Is Apex Metric good for beginners?

Yes. Choose "Let Apex decide" during setup and you start at Level 1 with simple, safe weights. The coach explains every exercise, and your level rises as you get stronger.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Everything works on your iPhone. With an Apple Watch you can log sets from your wrist and see live heart rate.

What happens after the 3 free months?

Nothing is charged. You move to the free plan automatically and keep your plan, workouts and history.

Does it work with my InBody or smart scale?

Yes. If your scale's app saves to Apple Health (InBody, Withings, Renpho, Eufy and most others do), new weigh-ins appear in Apex automatically.

Is it available in Portuguese and Spanish?

Yes — the app, the voice coach and this website are available in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

Is it on Android?

The Android version is in development and shares the same Apex Club, so friends on iPhone and Android can train in the same crews.
