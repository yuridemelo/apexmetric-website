Getting started

  • Set-up: answer the questions on first launch. Change anything later in Settings (gear icon at the top of each tab).
  • Your first workout: tap Start workout on My Plan, then Start set and Finish set for each set. The rest timer starts by itself.
  • The guided tour runs once per screen. Replay it in Settings › Show the app tour again.

Workouts

  • Change weight or reps: tap the number and type, or use + and −. Tap Done above the number pad to close it.
  • Barbell and machine loading: for barbells you enter only the plates; Apex adds the bar. Change the bar in the ··· menu › Bar weight.
  • Swap an exercise: ··· › Swap exercise. Equipment busy? ··· › Do it later.
  • Voice coach: Settings › Sound & voice. Tap 🔊 next to an exercise to hear how to do it again.
  • Apple Watch: open Apex Metric on the watch after starting a workout on your iPhone.

Backup, import and export

  • Backup: Settings › Backup & your data. Your workouts are backed up to your own iCloud automatically, encrypted.
  • New phone or reinstalled the app: sign in with the same Apple ID and tap "Restore my plan and workouts" on the first set-up screen.
  • From another workout app: export a CSV from that app and import it in Settings › Backup & your data.
  • Export: create a spreadsheet of your workouts and body data in the same screen.

Subscription

  • Free period: Premium is free for your first 3 months. Nothing is charged afterwards.
  • Restore purchases: Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases.
  • Cancel on iPhone: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Apex Metric.
  • Cancel on Android: Google Play › profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

Privacy and Face ID

  • Lock Apex with Face ID (iPhone, iOS 18+): touch and hold the app icon › Require Face ID.
  • Android: use Secure Folder (Samsung), Private Space (Pixel) or your phone's App lock.
  • Apple Health permissions: Health app › Sharing › Apps › Apex Metric.

Community and safety

  • Private account: on by default. Change it in Profile › Edit profile.
  • Report a post or comment: tap ··· on the post, or swipe the comment.
  • Block someone: ··· on their post › Block. They can no longer see your content or contact you.
